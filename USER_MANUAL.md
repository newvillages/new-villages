## 1. Getting Started

### 📝 Creating an Account
1. Open the **New Villages** website and click the **Sign up** button in the top right corner.
2. Select your account type:
   * **Regular Member**: If you want to join existing groups and participate in local events.
   * **Community Leader**: If you want to create and manage local community circles.
   * **Organization**: If you are a business or non-profit providing services and events.
3. Fill in your details (Full Name, Email, Password, Country, City, and Language) and click **Sign up**.
4. Check your email inbox for a verification link and click it to activate your account.

### 🔑 Logging In
1. Click the **Log in** button in the top right corner of the home page.
2. Enter your email and password, then click **Log In**.
3. If you ever forget your password, click the **Forgot password?** link, enter your email, and follow the link sent to your inbox to set a new one.
4. *Tip*: If you ever want to return to the landing home page, click the **Back to Home** button above the login form.

## 2. For Regular Members (Discovering & Connecting)

### 🏡 Your Dashboard
When you log in, you will be taken to your **Dashboard**. Here you can see:
* A greeting banner showing your upcoming community events.
* A list of communities you have joined.
* A list of upcoming events.
* Recent activity feeds.

### 🔍 Finding and Joining Communities
1. Click the **Communities** tab in the top navigation bar.
2. In the **Discover** tab, you will see a list of active community circles. Use the search bar to find groups that match your interests.
3. Click the **Join** button next to a community:
   * **Public Groups**: You will join immediately.
   * **Private Groups**: A request is sent to the group's leader for approval.

### 📅 RSVP to Events
1. To view all events, click on the **Calendar** or navigate to an event through the dashboard.
2. Inside the event page, choose your attendance status:
   * Click **Going** if you plan to attend.
   * Click **Interested** if you are undecided.
   * Click **Declined** if you cannot make it.
3. Events will show if they are online (with a Zoom/Meet link) or physical (with a map address).

### 💬 Sending Direct Messages
1. Click the **Messages** link in the navigation menu.
2. Select a member or leader from your chat list on the left to start a conversation.
3. Type your message in the chat box and press enter or click the send button.
4. *Note*: You can only message community leaders or members within your circles. You can also block or report users for safety concerns if needed.

## 3. For Community Leaders (Managing Your Circles)

### 🛡️ Accessing the Leader Portal
If you are registered as a **Community Leader**, you will see a **Leader Portal** option in the top navigation bar.

### 👥 Managing Members
1. Go to the **Leader Portal** and select your community from the dropdown list (if you manage more than one).
2. Under the **Member Roster** tab, you can view the names, roles, and join dates of all active members.
3. Under the **Join Requests** tab, you will see requests to join your private community. Click **Approve** to welcome them, or **Reject** to dismiss the request.

### 📢 Publishing Announcements
1. In the **Leader Portal**, click the **Publish Announcement** tab.
2. Select your community, type your news or update in the message box, and click **Broadcast Message**.
3. This post will immediately appear on the feeds of all members in your community.

### 📅 Managing Events
1. In the **Leader Portal**, select the **Manage Events** tab.
2. To add a new event:
   * Click **Create Event**.
   * Fill out the event title, category, date, time, and details.
   * Specify if it's online (input a link) or physical (input an address).
   * Click **Publish Event**.
3. To remove an event, click the **Trash icon (Delete)** next to the event in the list and click confirm.

### 📈 Viewing Analytics
1. In the **Leader Portal**, click the **Analytics** tab.
2. Review your community's vital stats: total registered members, upcoming events, and pending requests.

## 4. For Organizations (Creating Your Profile)

### 🏢 Setting Up Your Profile
1. Click **Org Page** in the navigation menu.
2. Fill out your organization's Name, Description, Logo, services provided, and contact email.
3. Click **Create Page** to make your organization visible to the platform's members.

### 📅 Publishing Organization Events
Organizations can host public events to connect with the local community.
1. Click the **Create** button in the header.
2. Set the target to your Organization, choose the event type, select a date/time, and fill out the details.
3. Click **Publish Event** to share it on the public calendar.

## 5. Getting Help (Contacting Support)

If you have questions, run into issues, or want to submit feedback to the moderation board:
1. If you are **logged out**, click the **Contact** link in the header menu or the footer.
2. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, **Subject**, and **Message**.
3. Click **Send Message**. 
4. Our support team will get back to your email within 24 hours.
